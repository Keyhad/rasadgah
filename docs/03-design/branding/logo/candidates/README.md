## 1. Purpose

Records the logo concepts considered for Rasadgah and why Concept A was selected. Version 2.0 replaces all earlier candidates that were inherited from another product.

## 2. Design Principles

- The mark shall express the product name: *rasadgah* is Persian for "observatory".
- The mark shall be recognisable at 16 × 16 px.
- The mark shall use only the brand palette ([DES-011](../../color-palette/color-palette.md)): navy tile, sky-blue accent, amber star.
- Flat geometry only: no gradients, shadows or text in the icon.

## 3. Candidates

### 3.1 Concept A — Dome and rising bars

File: [concept-a-dome-bars.svg](concept-a-dome-bars.svg)

An observatory dome sits on a base line. Three ascending bars inside the dome represent KPIs. The telescope tube points to an amber star, which represents the target.

### 3.2 Concept B — Lens and trend

File: [concept-b-lens-trend.svg](concept-b-lens-trend.svg)

A magnifying lens over an amber trend line.

### 3.3 Concept C — Gauge horizon

File: [concept-c-gauge-horizon.svg](concept-c-gauge-horizon.svg)

A half-circle gauge with a needle, a progress arc and a star on the horizon.

## 4. Evaluation Matrix

Scores from 1 (poor) to 5 (strong).

| Criterion                                    | A      | B      | C      |
| -------------------------------------------- | ------ | ------ | ------ |
| Expresses "observatory"                      | 5      | 3      | 2      |
| Expresses KPIs and targets                   | 5      | 4      | 4      |
| Legibility at 16 px                          | 4      | 4      | 3      |
| Distinctiveness from generic analytics icons | 5      | 2      | 2      |
| **Total**                                    | **19** | **13** | **11** |

## 5. Decision

Concept A is selected. The production assets are in [logo/](../README.md). Concepts B and C are kept as rejected alternatives.
